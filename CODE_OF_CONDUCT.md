# Code of conduct

Designboard is an open source project where contributors should be able to participate without
harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical risk.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Help keep discussions focused and useful to maintainers and contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publishing private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these rules.
Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options at
[juanlebrero.com](https://juanlebrero.com). Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as
possible.
